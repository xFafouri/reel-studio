# Script and hook

The script is approved BEFORE any voice or picture is made. Order of approvals:
**script → voice (listen) → stills (layout) → full video**. Never skip a gate; never start a build from a bare "ok".

## Facts first
- Every fact comes from a named source you opened (journal page, abstract, official health/science pages). Keep the
  source list next to the script. Numbers are copied, never rounded into something new, never invented.
- Name the study in voice AND on screen: "a study in the journal Brain, 2019" + small "Postuma et al. · Brain · 2019".
- The verdict on screen must match the evidence (true / false / partly). Drama is fine, a false verdict is not.
- Imagined or illustrative lines say so ("imagine…", "maybe…").

## Hook (the first 3 seconds decide everything)
- Frame 0 already shows the hook text; something moves in the first 0.5 s.
- ≤ 5 words, everyday language: the sentence the viewer has actually heard or thought, not a title.
- The hook title IS the voice, word by word as said (not a separate summary).
- Open a loop you close later ("the answer is at the end"); keep every promise the hook makes.
- Write 5–6 hooks and pick one; when copying a successful video's structure, copy its timing, not its words.

## Length and pace
- Speech: English ≈ 130–150 words a minute, Arabic ≈ 100–110. Time the first voice take before planning scenes.
- Story Reel 30–40 s; explainer 45–75 s; demo 30–40 s. Cut a scene before speeding the voice up.
- Dead air kills retention: cut pauses > 0.4 s inside a line, 0.25 s of picture before each line, 0.3 s after
  (hold ≈ 1 s after a scene the viewer must read).

## Script file layout (one row per scene)
```
| # | Voice (exactly what is said) | On screen | Picture / animation | SFX |
| 1 | Don't drink water with your meal! | (hook text = the voice) | clip 1, frame 0 | — |
```
When someone else wrote the script: keep their exact version as a backup before editing, change only what's
needed, and list each change with its reason.

## Ending
- Story Reels: no logo, last frame ≈ first frame (loop).
- Explainers: a call to action + an optional short logo outro.
- A share line ("send this to someone who…") usually beats "follow".
